Honest caveats

  • Lossy full-mix source: the mid lift raises everything at 500–2k, not just the vocal — at the PC, put the bell on the vox group, not the master.
  • Crest reads ~1 dB lower on previews than the bounce — the −1 dBTP limiter catching the old overs.
  • S3's arc is one opinion of the shape — treat it as a demo of whether, not exactly where.
