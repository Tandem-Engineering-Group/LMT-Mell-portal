Honest caveats

  • Source is lossy and already clipped — declip repairs the samples, not the crush. Crest 9.4 is the post-processing ceiling; the session render is the real fix.
  • Band Δs are relative shares — big bass boosts dilute other bands' numbers even when their absolute level rose.
  • The vocal ride is a band ride on a near-mono mix — it lifts keys sharing 1–4 kHz too.
