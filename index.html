The diagnosis, not the current state. The one red flag was sub — this song sat an octave above his released low end (40–60 Hz). Tonight's 808 layer closes it: sub now −4.3 dB (hear it in the A/B up top). Everything else was already on or above his release profile.

What's in the studio session

  • Console-grouped for the rig: four group tracks — BEAT · VOX · STEMS · REFS — each on its own Xone fader (1 · 2 · 4 · 6), FULL MIX on 5. All vocals ride one fader; the UF8 strips read the group names.
  • Restored the sub: the beat's own 808 layer was being strangled by a 55 Hz low-cut sitting on the stems — killing the 42 Hz fundamental. Cut disabled, level set, low-passed at 80 Hz to keep it clean. Sub −22 → −4 dB.
  • Every vocal lane trimmed to match — measured per-lane loudness, each pulled to the median (±5 dB), so no take jumps out.
  • Lanes relabeled by content: LEAD A/B (the comp takes), VOX A–D (support), ADLIB 1–4, STAB, HARM — fits the scribble strips.
  • Fixed the timing landmine: the vocals ride the beat sped +2 st; all 298 beat-MIDI clips retimed and transposed so the stems finally sit on the record.
  • Master bus: glue + limiter at −1 dBTP — the FL bounce's clipping can't recur. Parked live on channel 6: 5 OwnLane spillover takes cut the same night, plus Lost Files and Equity as A/B references.
