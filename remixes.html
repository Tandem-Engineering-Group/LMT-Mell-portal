Honest caveats

  • Source is a lossy full mix — the vocal ride is a band ride, not a fader on vocal tracks.
  • Mix 1 keeps a touch more floor than the original on purpose — full-original sub recreates the no-floor flaw.
  • All four run ~1–2 dB brighter in air than the bounce — a side effect of undoing the render's darkness. Judge by ear.
  • Mix 4's motion is pump/breathe, not literal warping — that needs the session.

The real fix, at the PC

  • Mute channel 6 (parked refs) before every export.
  • Vox group +4.5 dB; new sub/808 down 8–10 dB.
  • Rebuild presence/air at the source, not with EQ after.
  • Swap in the SIMPLIFIED MIDI (7 tracks for 11 files).
  • Master stays: glue + limiter −1 dBTP, land ≈−11.8 LUFS.
  • A/B against MIX 2 (safe) or MIX 3 (forward).
