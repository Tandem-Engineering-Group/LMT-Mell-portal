Which one to send

  • MIX A — VOCAL FORWARD. The most words-per-listen. Biggest presence lift, tightest low end, most dynamic. Send this if the note was “I can’t hear him.”

Uploading — read this bit

  • Upload the (master 24bit).wav from each song’s 05 Ableton Exports folder. That is the delivery asset.
  • The players above are mp3 previews, trimmed 1–2 dB below the WAV because mp3 encoders overshoot the ceiling. Verified under −1 dBTP after decoding too.
  • Apple normalises playback with Sound Check — it will pull these toward −16 LUFS by itself. Delivering at −10 to −11.5 keeps the dynamics his catalogue has been trending toward since In Yo Dreams.
  • Not formal “Apple Digital Masters” for A01/A02 — that badge needs a 24-bit/88.2 kHz+ source and those two only exist as mp3 bounces. Still fully upload-ready. A WAV bounce of the instrumentals from Mell removes the ceiling.
Chain per mix: rumble filter → per-song corrective EQ from the measured faults → activity-keyed vocal presence lift + de-mud → de-esser → widening above 250 Hz (lows stay mono) → glue → 4×-oversampled true-peak limiter. Loudness by BS.1770 gated integration; true peak measured at 4× oversampling on the WAV and on the decoded preview.
